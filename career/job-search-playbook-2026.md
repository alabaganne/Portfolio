# Finding a High-Paying Full-Time Job in Europe or the US — 2026 Playbook

**Goal:** Land a new high-paying, full-time software engineering role with a US or European company.

**Your starting position (use this as leverage):**
- 5+ years full-stack (React, Next.js, Node.js, Python/FastAPI, Laravel).
- Current, in-demand **AI experience** — RAG, LLM integration, DSPy, document processing (NORA / Wequity).
- Healthcare + legal-tech domain depth (regulated industries pay more).
- **Upwork Top Rated, 100% JSS** — proof you deliver for Western clients.
- Already paid internationally via **Deel** — you know how cross-border employment works.
- Based in Tunisia → your cost base is low, so even "mid" Western salaries are high-paying for you.

---

## Step 0 — Decide your employment model first (this shapes everything)

You won't get a typical "relocate to SF" job easily without sponsorship. Pick the realistic paths and target them:

1. **Remote full-time via EOR (Employer of Record)** — *highest probability.* Companies hire you through Deel/Remote.com/Oyster; they handle Tunisian compliance. Target companies that explicitly say "remote, anywhere" or "remote EMEA."
2. **Remote full-time as a contractor** — you invoice (you already do this on Upwork). Often the fastest to close; negotiate a higher rate to offset no benefits.
3. **Relocation + visa sponsorship** — slower, but real for senior engineers. EU is more accessible than US:
   - **Germany** (EU Blue Card / Opportunity Card), **Netherlands** (highly skilled migrant), **Portugal/Estonia** (tech/digital-nomad friendly), **Ireland**.
   - US: H-1B is a lottery; **O-1** (extraordinary ability) is possible long-term if you build a public record; **L-1** if a company transfers you.

> Decide: are you optimizing for **money now (remote)** or **relocation later**? Do remote now; build the profile that unlocks relocation/O-1 in parallel.

## Step 1 — Set a target salary (know your number)

Anchor high and let the market correct you. Rough 2026 remote ranges for a senior full-stack/AI engineer working with Western companies:

| Model | Realistic range (USD) | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Remote contractor (EMEA timezone) | $45k–$90k+ | Higher if AI-heavy; bill as a rate ($35–$70/hr). |
| Remote FTE via EOR | $60k–$120k+ | "Remote-anywhere" startups pay closer to US bands. |
| Relocation FTE (EU) | €65k–€110k | Germany/NL/Ireland senior bands. |
| Relocation FTE (US, sponsored) | $130k–$200k+ | Rare without sponsorship; long game. |

Use **Levels.fyi**, **Glassdoor**, and remote salary reports to refine per company. Always give a **range with a high floor**, and never name your number first if you can avoid it.

## Step 2 — Sharpen your positioning (this is where the money is)

Generic "full-stack developer" competes with everyone. Position as a **specialist**:

- **Primary angle: "AI-product full-stack engineer"** — you ship RAG/LLM features end-to-end, not just call an API. This is the hottest, highest-paid niche in 2026.
- **Secondary angle: regulated domains** — healthcare + legal tech (compliance, data integrity, multilingual document processing).
- One-line pitch to reuse everywhere:
  > "Full-stack engineer who ships production AI features — RAG knowledge bases, LLM document pipelines, and the web apps around them — for healthcare and legal-tech teams."

## Step 3 — Fix your assets before applying (1 week)

- **Resume:** keep it to 1–2 pages, lead with AI + impact metrics (you already have `resume.md`). Tailor a version per role — mirror the job's keywords for ATS.
- **LinkedIn:** headline = your specialist pitch, not "Software Engineer @ X". Turn on **#OpenToWork (recruiters-only)**. Fill skills, get 3–5 recommendations (ask Upwork clients + colleagues).
- **GitHub:** pin your best repos; make sure your profile README (`github-profile-readme`) reflects the AI angle. Green contribution graph helps.
- **Portfolio (alabaganne.com):** your live demos on subdomains are a strong differentiator — most candidates have nothing live. Keep it polished.
- **A short "proof" artifact:** 1 case study / blog post on the NORA RAG system or a side project. Public proof of AI work fuels both jobs *and* a future O-1.

**Highest-yield channel — referrals & inbound:**

## Step 5 — Apply with volume *and* quality (weeks 1–6)

- **Target ~10–15 quality applications/week**, not 100 spray-and-pray.
- For each: tailor the resume keywords, write a 3–4 sentence note connecting your AI/domain experience to their product.
- Prioritize roles posted in the **last 7 days** (much higher response rate).
- Track everything in a simple sheet: Company · Role · Date · Channel · Status · Next action. (You can keep it in `job-applications/`.)
- Aim for a funnel: ~100 applications → ~10–15 screens → ~4–6 onsites → 1–3 offers.

---

## Step 6 — Interview prep (run in parallel from week 1)

- **DSA/coding:** LeetCode — ~75 core problems (NeetCode 150 is the efficient list). 30–45 min/day.
- **System design:** essential at senior/high-pay level. Study "Designing Data-Intensive Applications" + system-design primers; be ready to design a RAG pipeline, a multi-tenant SaaS, a video platform (you've built these — use *your* projects as examples).
- **Behavioral:** prepare 6–8 STAR stories from Retain/Wequity/freelance (impact, conflict, ownership, a hard bug, a shipped feature).
- **AI-specific:** be ready to explain RAG architecture, chunking/embeddings, evals, hallucination mitigation, cost/latency tradeoffs — your real edge.
- **Mock interviews:** Pramp / interviewing.io to remove nerves.

---

## Step 7 — Negotiate (don't skip — this is the highest $/hour you'll ever earn)

- Never accept the first number. Ask: "What's the range for this role?"
- Always try to have **2+ offers** in play — competing offers move comp the most.
- Negotiate the **whole package**: base, bonus, equity, signing bonus, PTO, equipment budget, and **contractor rate vs EOR** (rate should be ~20–30% higher to offset no benefits).
- For relocation roles: get visa/relocation support **in writing**.
- Silence is a tool. After they give a number, pause, then counter with a specific higher number + a one-line justification.

---

## Step 8 — Long game (do these now, pay off in 6–18 months)

- Build a **public record** (talks, posts, OSS, blog) — unlocks inbound recruiters now and an **O-1 visa** later.
- Keep one impressive **AI side project** live and evolving.
- Nurture relationships with every recruiter/hiring manager even after a "no" — the next role comes from them.
