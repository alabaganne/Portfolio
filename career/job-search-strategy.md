# Job Search Strategy

**Goal:** Full-time remote role, quickly.
**Open to:** US and Europe based companies.
**Profile edge:** Tunisia-based (CET timezone), 5 years experience, Top Rated on Upwork, already working remotely with US startups, strong full-stack JS/React, and a portfolio of live, solo-built products.

## Top 3 platforms (focus here)

1. **Wellfound (AngelList Talent)** — #1 target. Startups, lots of full-time remote roles hiring globally, evaluated on shipped work. Set profile to "remote, open to opportunities" and apply directly to founders.
2. **LinkedIn** — Essential for full-time. Turn on "Open to Work" (remote) and use "Easy Apply." Where EU companies recruit most actively. Headline: "Full-Stack Engineer · React/Next.js · Remote."
3. **We Work Remotely + RemoteOK** — Highest-volume remote-only boards. Filter to "Worldwide/Anywhere"; skip "US-only timezone" posts.

## Add these for my specific case

- **Y Combinator Work at a Startup** — US startups, many hire async/globally full-time. Same type of company as Retain and Nora, where I already thrive.
- **EU-focused boards** (EU Remote Jobs, Honeypot, Arc.dev) — Europe's timezone overlap with Tunisia (CET) is excellent, so EU companies see me as effectively local. A real edge worth leaning into.

## Speed strategy

- **Keep Upwork warm.** Don't drop Top Rated status while job-hunting. It's income and a fallback, and some clients hire full-time.
- **Lead with live products.** Most applicants have GitHub repos; I have deployed, real-user products built solo. Put 2-3 live links at the top of every application.
- **Tailor one line per region:** emphasize CET timezone overlap for EU roles, and "5 years working async with US startups" for US roles.
- **Volume on the right 3 beats scattershot.** Aim for ~5 quality, tailored applications a day on Wellfound + LinkedIn + one board, not 30 generic ones.
