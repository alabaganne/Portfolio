# Business & App Ideas

All business, product, and side-project ideas in one place.

## App ideas

- **MenuMate** — digital restaurant menus / dashboard.
- **Appointment / reservation system** (Reservily / Reservation.tn) — any service provider creates an account and shares a link so clients pick a reservation time. Targets: car washes (e.g. Saif Lavage), doctors, dentists, lawyers.
- **Stagi.tn** — evolve the PFE app into a platform for students to find internships.
- **Home food app** — anyone at home can open a home restaurant and sell food; we manage delivery and client orders.
- **AlloPro** — service marketplace for electricians, plumbers, and any real-life service provider.
- **Tenant finder** / **real estate selling**.
- **Freelance escrow**.
- **Tenders platform** — companies/government publish projects/contracts; businesses/freelancers submit bids. Most tender platforms are slow, ugly, not mobile-friendly, with bad search/filtering and poor notifications — the opportunity is to build something modern and better than competitors. (See also `tuneps.tn` opportunity below.)
- **SaaS-for-SaaS marketing tool** — helps SaaS owners market their ideas: post to all social media at once, generate blog/social posts.

### Bigger / favorite ideas
- **ANKI Plus** (favorite) — better design + AI that summarizes a PDF/text and auto-creates ANKI cards. Market-validated (ANKI is popular).
- **Social media management AI** — manage all social content from one place (post, edit, delete, multi-post, schedule). Generate daily content (text, emojis, hashtags) and auto-share to pages.
- **ideas.app** (a.k.a. Ideas Palace / Ideas Marketplace / geniusidea) — people share app ideas they want built, with an upvote system to surface great ones. Pitch: solo devs find their next idea and validate it (more upvotes = more demand). Competitor: appsumo.com.
- **Problemy** — people share problems so developers can turn them into profitable solutions. Monetization: free until a large problem database is built, then monthly subscription. Could run alongside ideas.app for more marketing reach.
- **YouTube video summarizer** (WatchLess / SumItUp / TooLongToWatch) — use a transcript API, summarize with an LLM, highlight key takeaways, cache results to avoid re-summarizing.

### Store / content ideas
- Stores: Protein, Jewelry, Cookies ("Brooks"), Cakes.
- Faceless YouTube channel **"Wise man once said"** — a wise man telling a story/quote over inspiring music; good stories should grab attention.
